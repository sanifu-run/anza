output, diagnostics, and context. Setup requires a real terminal and records
explicit consent before contacting the chat provider. The end-to-end fixture
uses a subprocess with an isolated temporary HOME and workspace, synthetic
wizard/review input, and loopback-only release/download servers. It exercises
setup, plan, review, apply, repair, doctor, exercise, update, diagnostics,
uninstall, inspect, and the no-argument guided flow through the production
`NewApp` handlers. The guided flow completes interview, plan, typed review,
apply, login guidance, doctor, and exercise. A review-skip case reports partial
readiness and leaves no approval for apply. The fake release service receives
one request; no download is needed for the approved artifact-free plan. The
fixture confirms the diagnostics export stays under the temporary HOME and the
separate workspace remains empty.

credentials require interactive consent and a terminal-masked prompt; the CLI
disables launch's environment lookup. Tests use an in-memory fake native
backend, synthetic prompt, and fake runner, and prove a conflicting environment
value is ignored and secrets do not enter output or argv. Noninteractive mode
fails with guidance before prompting.
